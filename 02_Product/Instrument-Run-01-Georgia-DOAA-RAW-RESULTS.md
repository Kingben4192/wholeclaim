## Instrument break log

Every point where the instrument failed, or where a finder could plausibly enter something wrong.

**Break 1 — The root-cause field has no value for any real finding in this sample.**
R1–R5 were derived entirely from NYC equipment-inventory audits. Every Georgia county finding retrieved concerns financial reporting, budget compliance, segregation of duties, cash handling, or federal awards reporting. A finder working these sources has no valid code to enter for any row. **Mis-entry risk: high.** A finder wanting to be helpful will stretch a code — "segregation of duties" superficially resembles R4 custody — and produce a false positive. Nothing in the frozen instrument prevents this.

**Break 2 — The designated stress test could not be performed.**
The instruction required at least one partial-federal-funding-scope finding to stress the P3 scope field. **No such finding exists in this sample.** Zero Georgia county findings retrieved involve a physical inventory count at all, partial or otherwise. Entry 7 was entered as the nearest analogue and is explicitly *not* a valid test — it is federal awards *reporting*, not a scoped count. **The P3 scope field remains untested against real data.**

**Break 3 — `PA / IA / neither` and `Designation as-of date` cannot be populated from the audit source.**
These require a separate FEMA lookup. A finder working from an audit PDF has no path to them. Populated 0 of 10. **Mis-entry risk: high** — the two-source dependency is not stated on the form, so a finder may leave them blank (indistinguishable from "neither"), guess from general knowledge, or skip the row. The instrument does not distinguish *not looked up* from *looked up, no designation*.

**Break 4 — No field records the audit's own severity classification.**
Sources distinguish material weakness from significant deficiency, and DeKalb reported material weaknesses while Fannin reported significant deficiencies. The instrument captures none of this, so all rows appear equally weighted.

**Break 5 — No field records finding status or recurrence.**
Jasper 2022-001 and 2022-002 are marked "Unresolved"; Fannin 2022-006 is "Not Corrected"; Fannin 2022-001 is "Corrected." Recurrence is arguably the strongest available signal of a real unsolved problem, and the instrument discards it. Prior-year findings also have no place in the table — they were logged in the section above only because I chose to.

**Break 6 — "Finding (as written)" has no defined boundary.**
Sources structure findings as CRITERIA / CONDITION / CONTEXT / CAUSE / EFFECT / RECOMMENDATION. The field does not say which part to enter. I entered Condition, plus Criteria where Condition alone was unintelligible — **that was my judgment call, and it is exactly the kind of undocumented decision that makes two finders produce different tables.**

**Break 7 — One retrieved county produced no locatable findings section.**
Dade County was retrieved and scanned; no findings section was located in the extracted text. The instrument has no way to record "source retrieved, findings not located" as distinct from "no findings exist" or "not yet examined."

**Break 8 — Extraction is not reproducible by a non-technical finder.**
Two source PDFs (Fannin, Habersham) were AES-encrypted and required an additional library before text could be extracted. A finder without those tools would have recorded them as inaccessible. This is a method fragility, not a field-set defect, but it affects any attempt to scale the run.
