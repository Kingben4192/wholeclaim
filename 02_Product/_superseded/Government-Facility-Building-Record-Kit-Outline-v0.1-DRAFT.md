## 0. Format decisions

Shared with the line per Decision #112 (regulatory posture, visual direction, record architecture, boundary language); capacity decided for this kit specifically:

- **Capacity:** 25 building systems or major equipment items per binder, one facility per binder — decided for this kit, not inherited (Decision #112). Justification: a mid-size government building carries roughly that many major systems, so one binder maps to one facility without continuation for the typical case. Campus and multi-building sites use one binder per building.
- **Regulatory posture:** structural alignment, no citations. Field names mirror standard federal facility-management practice; the kit cites no regulation and claims no conformance.
- **Visual system:** Pine / Ink / Paper. Stamp Red retained only for deficiency and life-safety sections where alarm is functionally correct. Brass Amber dropped from this line.

**Boundary language** — the "what this kit is and is not" front matter from Kit 1 §1 applies verbatim and is not restated here: not an official form, not a compliance certification, not a substitute for agency policy, not legal or audit advice.

**Scope note:** this kit documents the facility and its systems. Maintenance *execution* belongs to Kit 3 and incidents belong to Kit 4; §9 and §10 below cross-reference rather than duplicate them.

## 1. Product definition

**Buyer:** facility manager, building operations officer, or administrative officer responsible for a single government-occupied building or campus.

**Job it does:** hold a complete standing record of one facility — its spaces, systems, condition, inspections, and life-safety information — in a form that survives staff turnover and answers a building review without reconstruction.

**Unit of the binder:** one facility. Multi-building sites use one binder per building.

## 2. Section structure

### Front Matter — ~4 pp.
Title page · What this kit is and is not · How to use this kit · Recordkeeping conventions (permanent ink, single-line strike corrections initialed and dated, no erasure, date format, signature requirements, retention handling).

### Section 1 — Organization & Accountability Profile — ~2 pp.
Agency/component · Sub-office · Facility manager (name, title, contact) · Appointment date and delegation reference · Alternate/deputy · Supervisor of record · Fiscal year · Record period covered · Binder serial and volume number.

### Section 2 — Facility Master Profile — ~4 pp.
Facility name · Real property identifier · Street address · Ownership status (owned / leased / permit / license) · Lease or agreement reference and expiration · Year constructed · Gross square footage · Number of floors · Basement/sub-level (Y/N) · Construction type · Occupancy classification · Design occupant load · Primary use · Historic designation status · Site acreage · Utility service accounts · Prior facility identifiers.

### Section 3 — Space & Room Register — ~8 pp.
Landscape index, 4 rows per page. Room/space ID · Name or function · Floor · Net square footage · Occupancy type · Assigned organization · Assigned occupant or position · Finish/condition code · Access restriction level · Status (active / vacant / under renovation / closed).

### Section 4 — Building Systems & Equipment Register — ~8 pp.
Master index of the 25 units carried in the detail spreads. System ID · System type (HVAC / electrical / plumbing / fire suppression / fire alarm / elevator / roof / envelope / security / emergency generator / other) · Manufacturer · Model · Serial number · Installation date · Capacity or rating · Area served · Expected service life · Current condition code · Detail-spread page reference.

### Sections 5–8 — Per-System Record Spread — ~50 pp. (2 pp. × 25)
One two-page opening per system, carrying four sub-records so a system's full standing is visible in one view.

**5. System Identification & Specification** — System ID · Type and subtype · Manufacturer, model, serial · Installation date · Installing contractor · Capacity/rating · Fuel or power source · Area and rooms served · Design standard referenced · Replacement cost estimate · Expected end of service life.

**6. Inspection & Testing Record** — Inspection date · Inspection type (routine / annual / regulatory / acceptance / post-repair) · Inspector name and organization · Method · Result (pass / fail / conditional) · Deficiencies identified · Deficiency log cross-reference · Next inspection due · Inspector signature.

**7. Warranty & Service Coverage** — Warranty provider · Warranty start and end · Coverage scope · Exclusions · Service contract number · Contract provider and contact · Coverage period · Response-time commitment · Claim history · Warranty document reference.

**8. Modification & Alteration Record** — Date · Modification type (repair / upgrade / replacement / capacity change / decommission) · Description · Authorization document number · Approving official · Performed by · Cost · Permit reference · As-built drawing reference · Effect on capacity or service life · Return-to-service date.

### Section 9 — Facility Inspection Record — ~8 pp.
Whole-building inspections, distinct from per-system testing in §6. Inspection date · Inspection type (annual facility condition / safety / environmental / accessibility / insurance / regulatory) · Inspecting party and organization · Scope and areas covered · Overall condition rating · Findings summary · Deficiencies logged · Report reference · Next inspection due · Certifying signature.

### Section 10 — Deficiency & Corrective Action Log — ~8 pp.
Deficiency ID · Date identified · Identified by · Source (inspection / occupant report / system failure / audit) · Location (room/space ID) · Associated system ID · Description · Severity (life safety / urgent / routine / deferred) · Estimated correction cost · Assigned to · Corrective action taken · Kit 3 work-order cross-reference · Completion date · Verified by and date · Status.

### Section 11 — Modification, Renovation & Project Record — ~6 pp.
Building-level changes above the system level. Project name and number · Description and scope · Authorization reference · Kit 5 project cross-reference · Start and completion dates · Contractor · Cost · Spaces affected · Permits obtained · Inspections passed · As-built documentation reference · Acceptance signature.

### Section 12 — Emergency Information & Life Safety — ~6 pp.
Emergency contact roster (name, role, 24-hour contact) · Utility shutoff locations (water, gas, electrical, fire suppression) with room references · Fire alarm panel location · Sprinkler riser and FDC location · Emergency generator location, fuel type, capacity, test schedule · Evacuation assembly points · Occupant emergency plan reference and revision date · Last drill date and type · AED and first-aid locations · Hazardous material storage locations and inventory reference · Accessibility egress provisions · Local responding fire/police jurisdiction and contact.

### Section 13 — Photographic Record — ~10 pp.
Photo ID · Associated space or system ID · Date taken · Photographer · View or angle · Subject (condition / deficiency / modification / as-built / general) · Notes. Mounting pages with fixed photo wells and caption blocks.

### Section 14 — Supporting Documentation Index — ~6 pp.
Document number · Type (lease / drawing / as-built / permit / inspection report / test certificate / warranty / service contract / correspondence / photograph / emergency plan) · Document date · Source · Storage location (physical file / digital path) · Retention period · Disposal-eligible date · Associated space or system ID.

### Section 15 — Audit, Review & Certification — ~4 pp.
Review date · Reviewer name and organization · Review type (internal / IG / external audit / insurance / change-of-manager) · Scope · Findings · Corrective action and due date · Closure date · Signature. Periodic certification statement · Facility manager signature and date · Supervisor signature and date · Period covered.

### Appendices A–F — ~14 pp.
A. Condition code reference · B. System type definitions · C. Deficiency severity definitions · D. Retention schedule worksheet · E. Glossary and abbreviations · F. Blank continuation sheets for §3, §4, §10.

## 5. Open questions

1. **One binder per building** — confirm. Campus and multi-building sites are common in government; a per-campus variant may be the more natural buy.
2. **Two registers (§3 spaces, §4 systems) is a departure from Kit 1's single register.** It's justified — spaces and systems are genuinely different objects — but it's the first structural divergence in the line and worth ratifying deliberately.
3. **§12 Emergency Information may be the strongest standalone value in the whole line.** It is the section most likely to be pulled out and used daily. Worth asking whether it deserves to be its own product.
4. **Overlap with Kit 3** at §6 (inspection/testing) and §10 (deficiency → work order) is the tightest seam in the line. If a buyer owns both kits, the boundary must be obvious or they'll double-enter.
