What’s inside

Twelve sections, one record

Complete Sections 01 and 02 first. Everything after that is added over time rather than finished in one sitting.

  • Property Profile

    The one-page identity of your home. Most of it comes straight from your closing documents, your policy declarations page, and the county assessor’s record.

  • Photo & Video Baseline

    Photographs are the backbone of the record. The master shot list, the walkthrough script, and the storage rules that keep ten years of photos findable.

  • Master Home Inventory

    Room by room, everything of value: what it is, what identifies it, what it is worth, and whether a photo of it exists. One register page per room.

  • Major Systems Register

    The big things that age, fail, and cost real money. Roof, HVAC, water heater, plumbing, electrical — with install dates, installers and condition notes.

  • Appliance & Serial Log

    One table for every data plate in the house. When a recall notice, warranty claim, or theft report asks for the model and serial, the answer is on this page.

  • Receipts & Purchases

    Where the proof of purchase lives. Log significant purchases and note where the receipt itself is — paper file, email, or photo.

  • Warranty Tracker

    Every warranty you own, when it dies, and who answers the phone. Item, provider, coverage type, start and expiration dates, registration confirmed.

  • Repair & Service History

    The running log of everything fixed, serviced, or inspected — the single most valuable page when a problem turns out to be a repeat problem.

  • Improvements & Renovations

    Project-level records: what the project was, what it truly cost, who did it, and the paper that proves it. One card per project.

  • Important Documents

    The papers that define the property — and, critically, where each one physically or digitally lives. A location column for every item.

  • Contractors & Services

    The directory of everyone who works on the house, so the next call, quote, or reference check starts from your own notes.

  • Annual Review

    One pass a year keeps the whole record alive. The annual checklist, a five-year review log, and a record of what changed this year.

How it works

The 60-minute baseline

One hour gets the spine of the record in place. Depth comes later — one section a week and the whole record is built inside three months.

  1. 0:00–0:10

    Property Profile

    Fill in Section 01 from memory and your closing papers. Address, year built, square footage, construction type.

  2. 0:10–0:35

    Walk and shoot

    Phone in hand, follow the Section 02 shot list. Wide shots of every room, four exterior elevations, roof from the ground, electrical panel open, water heater label, HVAC label.

  3. 0:35–0:50

    Serials

    Photograph the data plate of every major appliance and system, then copy model and serial into Section 05.

  4. 0:50–1:00

    Back it up

    Move today’s photos into one dated album or folder. Copy it somewhere outside the house — cloud, or a drive kept elsewhere.
