# WholeClaim — Free Claim Grade Partner Program
### A one-page guide for restoration and repair contractors

---

## Give your customers a better start after a loss.

WholeClaim gives homeowners a free Claim Grade and helps them organize the information they already need — their policy, photos, timeline, and receipts — so they show up prepared when questions come.

---

## Why hand this to a customer?

When a homeowner is dealing with property damage, the paperwork problem starts immediately — and it usually lands on you.

You've seen it:
- You ask for the policy, the photos, the dates. You wait.
- The customer can't find what the adjuster asked for, and the delay looks like yours.
- Questions come up weeks later, and nobody has a clean record of what happened when.

A customer who starts with WholeClaim shows up organized. They know what information matters. Their photos, receipts, and timeline are in one place. When questions come up, they can answer them — instead of calling you to ask what you remember.

Spend less time helping customers find missing information, and more time helping them complete the work.

---

## What the homeowner gets — free

- A Claim Grade — a short assessment of how complete their claim documentation is right now, with specific steps to improve it
- A place to organize everything — policy details, photos, receipts, dates, and a running timeline of the loss
- A documentation checklist built around what their situation actually needs

---

## What it asks of them

About two minutes. The homeowner answers a short set of questions about their situation and documentation, then provides their name and email to receive their Claim Grade and results. That's the whole setup.

---

## What WholeClaim is — and isn't

WholeClaim is not a contractor referral service. We do not send work to contractors — including contractors who introduce homeowners to WholeClaim. Our role is simply to help homeowners organize their claim documentation.

That means when you hand a customer WholeClaim:
- Nobody is marketing other contractors to your customer
- Your customer's information is theirs, not a lead being resold
- You're offering a genuinely neutral tool, and it reads that way

WholeClaim helps homeowners organize their claim, document their loss, and prepare their evidence. It does not provide insurance advice, guarantee claim approval, or determine claim outcomes.
