This isn't a guess about what governments need. It's built from what audits actually found.

We reviewed audit findings from multiple Georgia counties and found the same failure patterns showing up independently, across different departments and different asset types: records that don't exist, identification that's too vague to match a physical item to a file, disposals with no closing documentation, and safeguarding gaps that leave custody unclear.

Those patterns — not assumptions about what a department "should" have — are what each kit is built to close.

This research is still developing. We've validated these patterns against a small number of counties so far, and we're continuing that work. We'd rather tell you that plainly than overstate what we know.
