*Plain-language notice for the non-insurance documentation service.*

The service operates under the working name **The Documentation Center**.

## 2. What this service is — and what it is not

This is a **documentation and organization service.** We take property and
asset records an organization already has, and we organize them into a
structured, retrievable record.

**This service is not:**

- Insurance, or any insurance-related service
- Legal advice, or legal services of any kind
- Accounting, bookkeeping, or financial-statement services
- An audit, an audit service, or a compliance determination
- An inspection, appraisal, engineering evaluation, or valuation
- Public adjusting, claims filing, claims adjusting, or negotiation with any
  insurer

We do not tell you what your records mean, whether they satisfy any
requirement, or what any authority will conclude from them. **We organize
documentation. Determining what your organization is required to have, and
whether these records satisfy it, remains yours.**

**We do not work on insurance claims.** If your need concerns an active or
anticipated insurance claim, this service is not the right fit and we will say
so rather than take the work.

## 3. What you send us

You send **documents and photographs relating to organizational property and
assets.** In practice that means things like:

- Purchase records, invoices, and receipts
- Warranty documents
- Photographs of buildings, equipment, and other assets
- Existing inventories, asset lists, or spreadsheets
- Maintenance and service records
- Insurance policy documents, where relevant to identifying an asset
- Prior inspection or condition reports

## 4. Please do not send us more than the work needs

**Send only what the requested work requires.** We ask you specifically **not**
to send:

- Employee personnel files, medical information, or benefits records
- Social Security numbers, or any government-issued identification numbers
- Bank account numbers, full payment card numbers, or account credentials
- Records concerning identifiable clients, patients, students, or service
  recipients
- Anything held under a confidentiality, security, or classification
  restriction that would prohibit sharing it with an outside party

If a document you need to send contains any of the above incidentally, **redact
it before sending.** If we notice unnecessary sensitive information in what you
send, we will tell you and ask you to resend a redacted copy.

**Check your own obligations first.** Your organization may have rules about
sending records to an outside party. Confirming that this is permitted for your
records is your responsibility, not ours — we have no way to know what governs
your organization.

## 5. Why we collect these documents

For one reason: **to perform the documentation work you have paid for.** We use
what you send to build the organized record that is the deliverable, and to
communicate with you about that work.

We do **not** use your documents to train any AI system, we do **not** sell or
share them for marketing, and we do **not** use them as examples, case studies,
testimonials, or promotional material. *(Public use of anything learned from
customer work is separately prohibited by Decision #24 and would require its
own authorization.)*

# Flags for the founder — resolve before this can be finalized

**1. Operating entity — not guessable.** WholeClaim LLC exists, but no decision
records it as the operating entity for a *non-insurance institutional* service.
`Terms-of-Service-DRAFT.md` and `Privacy-Policy-DRAFT.md` both leave this field
open on purpose, noting the deliberate separation from A&K Construction LLC and
that the structure "should be made with counsel, not assumed." **That reasoning
applies here with more force, not less** — this service takes payment from
institutions, which is a different posture from selling consumer software.

**2. Contact address — both candidates fail.**

- `support@getwholeclaim.com` — **confirmed non-deliverable** (no MX records).
  Note that `Privacy-Policy-DRAFT.md` line 13 claims deliverability was
  confirmed on 2026-07-24 while line 96 of the same file says it was not.
  **That file contradicts itself and should be corrected separately.** It is
  also the wrong product line regardless.
- `research@propertyevidenceresearch.org` — the right line and the right
  domain, but **deliverability is unverified** (recorded as such in
  `11_GovSite/README.md` item 3). A privacy notice whose contact address
  bounces is worse than one with no address.

**Neither may be used until a live send test passes.**

**3. Brand name is a working name under accepted risk.** "The Documentation
Center" is not cleared branding (#122). Putting it on a document customers rely
on is a heavier use than putting it on marketing copy, and it widens the
exposure #125's second amendment already recorded.

**4. This notice cannot be finalized before the intake stack exists.** Sections
6, 7 and 8 are the substance of a privacy notice, and all three are functions
of a vendor choice that has not been made. **Item 1 of this request is
therefore genuinely blocked by item 3, not merely sequenced after it.**

**5. Attorney review is not optional here.** #128 §7 makes a data-handling and
privacy review a prerequisite before any real customer documents are accepted.
#130 excludes the insurance buyer and so avoids the *licensing* question — it
does **not** avoid the *data-handling* one. Receiving a nonprofit's or a county
department's property records raises the same question the insurance case
raised, for the same reason: they are real documents belonging to a real third
party.
