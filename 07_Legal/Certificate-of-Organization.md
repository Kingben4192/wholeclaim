## What this record does and does not settle

**Settles:** Decision #18 (form a software entity separate from A&K
Construction LLC). The entity exists, is a distinct Georgia domestic LLC, and
liability separation from the construction entity is structurally in place.

**Does not settle:** Decision #17 (name clearance). Registering an entity name
with the Georgia Secretary of State and clearing a **trademark** are different
things. GA SOS checks only whether the name is distinguishable from other
*Georgia entity* names on its register. It does not search USPTO Classes 9, 42
or 36, does not assess the WorldClaim proximity question, and confers no
trademark rights. **#17 remains open, with counsel.**

**Sequencing note, recorded for accuracy rather than as criticism.** #18 was
originally gated "after #17". Formation occurred 07/24/2026 while #17 is still
open. The practical consequence: if trademark counsel returns an adverse
opinion on the WholeClaim mark, the entity name is already filed and an
amendment or name change at the state level would be part of the remedy. This
is a known, accepted position, not an oversight to be corrected retroactively
in the log.
